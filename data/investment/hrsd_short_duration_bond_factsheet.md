## Fund Overview

The Halvorsen Ridge Short Duration Bond Fund aims to provide current income with low volatility and a high degree of capital preservation. It invests primarily in investment-grade US dollar bonds with short maturities, including Treasuries, agency securities, corporate bonds and high-quality securitized debt.
