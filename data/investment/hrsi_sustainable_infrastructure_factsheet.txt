FUND OVERVIEW
The Halvorsen Ridge Sustainable Infrastructure Fund invests in listed
companies that own or operate essential infrastructure assets that support
the energy transition: renewable generation, electricity transmission,
water utilities, digital infrastructure and low-carbon transport.

FUND-SPECIFIC ESG EXCLUSIONS
This fund applies stricter exclusions than the firm-wide ESG Policy
(INV-ESG-001). In particular, the fund excludes any company deriving more
than 5% of revenue from thermal coal mining or coal-fired power generation.
The fund also targets a weighted average carbon intensity at least 30%
below that of its benchmark.
