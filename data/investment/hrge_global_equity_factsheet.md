## Fund Overview

The Halvorsen Ridge Global Equity Fund seeks long-term capital appreciation by investing in a concentrated portfolio of high-quality listed companies across developed and emerging markets. The fund follows a bottom-up, fundamentals-driven process with a bias toward companies that generate durable free cash flow and earn returns on invested capital above their cost of capital.
