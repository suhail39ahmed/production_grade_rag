## 1. Pooled Fund Fees

The total expense ratio (TER) of each share class is made up of the management fee paid to Halvorsen Ridge Capital Management LLC and administration and other operating expenses (custody, fund accounting, transfer agency, audit, legal and shareholder servicing). None of the pooled funds charges a performance fee.

| Fund | Share class | Fund code | Management fee | Administration and other expenses | Total expense ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global Equity | Class A | HRGE-A | 0.50% | 0.12% | 0.62% |
| Global Equity | Institutional | HRGE-I | 0.40% | 0.08% | 0.48% |
| Short Duration Bond | Class A | HRSD-A | 0.30% | 0.11% | 0.41% |
| Short Duration Bond | Institutional | HRSD-I | 0.20% | 0.08% | 0.28% |
| Sustainable Infrastructure | Class A | HRSI-A | 0.65% | 0.14% | 0.79% |
| Sustainable Infrastructure | Institutional | HRSI-I | 0.55% | 0.09% | 0.64% |

Fees accrue daily and are reflected in each share class's net asset value.

## 2. Short-term Trading Fee

To protect long-term shareholders from the costs of frequent trading, a **short-term trading fee of 2.00%** of the redemption amount is charged on shares of **HRGE and HRSI** redeemed within **60 calendar days** of purchase. The fee is paid into the fund, not to HRCM. **HRSD does not charge a short-term trading fee.**

## 3. Separately Managed Accounts (SMA)

Separately managed accounts are charged a tiered annual management fee on assets under management:

| Assets under management | Annual fee rate |
|---|---|
| First USD 25 million | 0.55% |
| Next USD 75 million (USD 25m – 100m) | 0.45% |
| Above USD 100 million | 0.35% |

- The **minimum annual fee for an SMA is USD 50,000**.
- SMA fees are billed **quarterly in arrears** based on the market value of the account on the last business day of the quarter.
- Worked example: an account of USD 60 million pays (25m × 0.55%) + (35m × 0.45%) = USD 137,500 + USD 157,500 = **USD 295,000 per year**.
