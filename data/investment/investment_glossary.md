This glossary explains terms used in Halvorsen Ridge Capital Management LLC ("HRCM") documents. Definitions are general; specific limits and values are set in the relevant policy documents.

**Active share** — The percentage of a fund's holdings that differs from its benchmark. An active share of 0% means the fund is identical to the benchmark; 100% means no overlap.

**AUM (assets under management)** — The total market value of assets managed on behalf of clients.

**Benchmark** — An index against which a fund's performance is measured, for example the MSCI ACWI Net Total Return Index for the Global Equity Fund.

**Drawdown** — The decline in value from a previous peak. HRCM risk rules use *relative* drawdown, meaning the fund's drawdown minus the benchmark's drawdown over the same period.

**Duration (effective)** — A measure of a bond portfolio's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A duration of 2 years means the portfolio's value would fall by about 2% if yields rose by 1 percentage point.

**DV01** — The change in a portfolio's value for a one basis point (0.01%) move in yields.

**Expected Shortfall (ES)** — The average loss in the worst outcomes beyond a given confidence level. Also called conditional VaR.

**Fund code** — HRCM's identifier for a fund share class, made up of the fund abbreviation and a class suffix (A for Class A, I for Institutional). Example: HRSD-I.

**NAV (net asset value)** — The value of a fund's assets minus its liabilities. NAV per share is NAV divided by shares outstanding.

**Rebalancing band** — The permitted drift of an asset class from its target weight before rebalancing is required.

**SFDR** — The EU Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation, which classifies funds by sustainability characteristics (for example Article 8 and Article 9).

**Short-term trading fee** — A fee charged on redemptions made soon after purchase, paid into the fund to protect remaining shareholders.

**Total expense ratio (TER)** — The annual cost of owning a fund as a percentage of its assets, including the management fee and other operating expenses. TER is not the same as the management fee.

**Tracking error** — The standard deviation of the difference between a fund's returns and its benchmark's returns. Ex-ante tracking error is a model forecast; ex-post tracking error is measured from realized returns.

**Value-at-Risk (VaR)** — An estimate of the loss that a portfolio is not expected to exceed over a given horizon at a given confidence level. For example, a 1-day 99% VaR of 2% of NAV means that on 99 days out of 100 the loss is expected to be less than 2% of NAV. See the current Risk Management Framework (INV-RMF-001) for HRCM's VaR limits.

**Watchlist** — HRCM's list of strategies or managers under enhanced review because of persistent underperformance or organizational change. See INV-IPS-001, Section 9.3.

**Yield to maturity (YTM)** — The annualized return an investor would earn by holding a bond to maturity, assuming all payments are made as scheduled.
