1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy sets out the rules for personal trading, the restricted list,
gifts and entertainment, and reporting obligations for all employees of
Halvorsen Ridge Capital Management LLC ("HRCM"). All employees are treated
as "access persons" for the purposes of this policy. Contractors with
access to portfolio holdings or trading systems are also covered.

2. PERSONAL TRADING

2.1 Pre-clearance
All personal trades in covered securities must be pre-cleared through the
ClearTrade system before the order is placed. Covered securities include
listed equities, corporate and municipal bonds, options, futures, ETFs and
closed-end funds. Open-end mutual funds not managed by HRCM, US Treasury
securities and money market funds are exempt.

A pre-clearance approval is valid only for the trading day on which it is
granted and expires at 4:00 PM Eastern Time that day. Orders not executed
by then require a new pre-clearance request.

2.2 Minimum holding period
Covered securities must be held for at least 30 calendar days. Profits
from any sale within 30 days must be disgorged to a charity chosen by
Compliance, unless the sale was pre-approved for hardship.

2.3 Blackout period
Employees may not trade a security within 7 calendar days before or after
any HRCM fund or client account trades that same security. ClearTrade
rejects requests that fall within the blackout window automatically.

2.4 Initial public offerings and private placements
Employees may not buy securities in initial public offerings. Private
placements require written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer.

2.5 Crypto-assets
Personal holdings of crypto-assets are permitted but must be reported in
the quarterly holdings report. Crypto-asset exchange-traded products are
covered securities and require pre-clearance.

2.6 HRCM funds
Employees investing in HRCM pooled funds must hold their shares for at
least 90 calendar days. The short-term trading fee in the Fee Schedule
(INV-FEE-001) also applies to employee investments.

3. RESTRICTED LIST

Compliance maintains a restricted list of issuers about which HRCM may
hold material non-public information or for which trading is otherwise
restricted. No employee or fund may trade a security of an issuer on the
restricted list. The list is confidential and is enforced through
ClearTrade and the order management system. Employees must notify
Compliance immediately if they believe they have received material
non-public information.

4. GIFTS AND ENTERTAINMENT

Employees may not accept gifts with a total value of more than USD 150 per
calendar year from any single counterparty, broker or vendor. Business
entertainment (meals, events) where the giver attends is permitted if it
is not lavish or frequent, and must be logged in the Gifts and
Entertainment register within 10 business days. Cash and cash equivalents
(including gift cards) may never be accepted.

6. VIOLATIONS AND SANCTIONS

  First violation ........ Written warning and retraining
  Second violation ....... USD 500 fine paid to charity and loss of
                           personal trading privileges for 90 days
  Third violation ........ Referral to senior management; may result in
                           termination of employment

Disgorgement of profits (Section 2.2) applies in addition to the sanctions
above.

7. CONTACT

Questions should be sent to the Compliance team. Urgent restricted-list
questions should be directed to the Chief Compliance Officer.
