## 1. Purpose

This framework defines the market risk limits, escalation procedures and monitoring responsibilities that apply to all pooled funds managed by Halvorsen Ridge Capital Management LLC ("HRCM").

## 2. Risk Governance

The Risk Committee, chaired by the Chief Risk Officer, meets monthly and reports to the Investment Committee quarterly. The Risk function is independent of portfolio management and reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

## 3. Value-at-Risk Limits

VaR is calculated daily using a historical simulation model with a two-year lookback window, at a 99% confidence level and a one-day horizon.

| Fund type | 1-day 99% VaR hard limit (% of NAV) |
|---|---|
| Equity funds (HRGE, HRSI) | 2.5% |
| Fixed income funds (HRSD) | 0.8% |

**Firm-wide aggregate VaR limit:** USD 95 million (1-day, 99%).

## 4. Drawdown Rules

Drawdown is measured as the decline in a fund's NAV per share from its trailing 12-month peak, relative to the decline in its benchmark over the same period.

| Relative drawdown | Required action |
|---|---|
| Greater than 12% | Mandatory review by the Risk Committee within 5 business days |
| Greater than 18% | Mandatory de-risking plan submitted to the Investment Committee |

## 5. Stress Testing

Stress tests are run **monthly** using historical scenarios (2008, 2020) and hypothetical scenarios defined by the Risk Committee. Results are included in the monthly Risk Committee pack.

## 6. Limit Breach Escalation

- Hard limit breaches must be notified to the Chief Risk Officer within **2 business days**.
- The portfolio manager must submit a remediation plan within 5 business days.
- Breaches are reported to the Investment Committee at its next quarterly meeting.

## 7. Liquidity Risk

Each fund must hold a liquidity buffer of at least 5% of NAV in cash or instruments that settle within two business days.

## 8. Model Validation

The VaR model is validated annually by an independent party. Back-testing results are reviewed quarterly.

## 9. Review

This framework is reviewed annually by the Risk Committee.
