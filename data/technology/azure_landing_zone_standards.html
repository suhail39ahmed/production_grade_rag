1. Purpose

These standards define how Azure subscriptions, networks and resources are organized and governed at Lumenvale Technologies Inc. They are enforced through Azure Policy assignments at the management group level. Exceptions require approval from the Cloud Architecture Board.

2. Management Group Hierarchy

mg-lumenvale (root)
├── mg-platform
│   ├── mg-connectivity   (hub networking, firewall, DNS)
│   ├── mg-identity       (domain services, identity tooling)
│   └── mg-management     (Log Analytics, automation, monitoring)
├── mg-landingzones
│   ├── corp              (internal workloads, private connectivity only)
│   └── online            (internet-facing workloads)
├── mg-sandbox            (experimentation, no connectivity to corp)
└── mg-decommissioned     (subscriptions pending deletion)

3. Allowed Regions

Resources may be deployed only in eastus2 (primary), centralus (disaster recovery) and westeurope (EU customer data). Deployment to any other region is denied by policy.

4. Naming Convention

Resource names follow the pattern <resource-type>-<workload>-<env>-<region>-<instance>, using these abbreviations:

ElementValues
Resource typerg (resource group), vnet, snet, kv (Key Vault), dbw (Databricks workspace), st (storage), afw (firewall)
Environmentdev, test, prod, dr
Regioneus2, cus, weu
InstanceThree digits, e.g. 001

Example: rg-dataplat-prod-eus2-001. Storage accounts cannot contain hyphens and are limited to 24 characters, so the hyphens are omitted (for example stlvlakeprodeus2).

5. Mandatory Tags

Every resource group and resource must carry these tags; deployments without them are denied:

  • owner — email of the accountable team
  • cost-center — finance cost center code
  • env — dev, test, prod or dr
  • data-classification — public, internal, confidential or restricted

6. Networking

  • Hub-and-spoke topology. The hub VNet in each region uses 10.0.0.0/16 (eastus2) and 10.1.0.0/16 (centralus).
  • Spoke VNets are allocated as /20 blocks from the 10.64.0.0/10 range by the Cloud Platform Team through the IP address management tool.
  • All egress to the internet passes through Azure Firewall Premium in the hub.
  • Public IP addresses on network interfaces are denied by policy in the corp landing zone.
  • Private endpoints are mandatory for Storage, Key Vault, Azure SQL, Databricks and Event Hubs. Public network access must be disabled on these services.

7. Security Baseline

  • Microsoft Defender for Cloud is enabled on all subscriptions.
  • Diagnostic logs from all resources are sent to the central Log Analytics workspace log-platform-prod-eus2-001.
  • Owner role assignments at subscription scope are limited to the Cloud Platform Team; workload teams receive Contributor through Privileged Identity Management with a maximum activation of 8 hours.

8. Cost Management

Each subscription has a monthly budget with alerts at 80%, 100% and 120% of the budget, sent to the owner tag address and to FinOps.

9. Sandbox Subscriptions

Sandbox subscriptions have a spending cap of USD 500 per month and are automatically deleted 90 days after creation unless the owner requests a single 30-day extension.
